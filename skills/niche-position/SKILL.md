# /niche-position

> **CEO / CMO Principle**: If you sell to everyone, you sell to no one.  
> *"The narrower the niche, the higher the conversion, the lower the acquisition cost, and the easier the moat."*

## Purpose & Objective
`/niche-position` takes the technical and functional reality uncovered by `/audit-product` and sharpens the product's positioning in the market. It defines:
- The exact **Niche Market** where this existing product can be the #1 choice.
- The **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)** with the highest willingness to pay and fastest time-to-close.
- The **Anti-Persona**: Who to explicitly reject or say "NO" to.
- The **Defensible Moat**: What protects this product from being copied overnight by an AI wrapper.

## When to Reach for It
- Product growth is stagnating because messaging is too broad or generic.
- Users are asking for conflicting features from different market segments.
- Overhauling landing page messaging, pitch decks, or marketing campaigns.
- Triggered by typing `/niche-position [optional context or target hypothesis]`.

---

## Strategic Framework: The Niche Crucible

1. **Step 1: Feature-Pain Mapping**
   - Which specific feature in the codebase delivers 80% of the customer value?
   - Who suffers the most acute, painful loss when that feature is absent?

2. **Step 2: Narrow the Segment**
   - Shift from *Broad Category* (e.g. "Invoice Generator") to *Defensible Niche* (e.g. "Automated Multi-Currency Invoicing for Stripe Micro-SaaS Founders").

3. **Step 3: Define Anti-Personas**
   - Explicitly identify user types that drain support, request enterprise custom features, and dilute product focus.
