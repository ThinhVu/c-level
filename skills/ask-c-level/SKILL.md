# /ask-c-level

> **Advisory Principle**: Fast advice from a multidisciplinary board saves months of costly trial and error.  
> *"When in doubt, consult the collective intelligence of Product, Growth, Tech, and Revenue before committing code."*

## Purpose & Objective
`/ask-c-level` is an interactive consultation skill. Whenever you face an ambiguous dilemma, customer feedback question, or sudden operational fork in the road, invoke `/ask-c-level <question>` to receive concise, multi-perspective executive counsel:
- 👑 **CEO Perspective**: Focus on vision, brand identity, and defensibility.
- 🧭 **CPO Perspective**: Focus on user experience, workflow simplicity, and product cohesion.
- 📈 **CMO Perspective**: Focus on distribution ease, messaging resonance, and viral vectors.
- 💰 **CRO Perspective**: Focus on pricing power, customer willingness to pay, and margin health.
- ⚙️ **CTO Perspective**: Focus on architectural simplicity, maintenance burden, and execution risk.

## When to Reach for It
- Wondering whether to respond to a feature request from an enterprise prospect.
- Considering adding a new third-party dependency vs building a lightweight custom script.
- Deciding between multiple pricing tier ideas.
- Unsure how to position a new feature on Twitter/X or ProductHunt.
- Triggered by typing `/ask-c-level <your question or dilemma>`.

---

## Output Format
Concise, bulleted executive synthesis:
1. **Multi-Perspective Table**: How each Chief views the problem.
2. **Consensus Verdict**: The unified C-Suite recommendation.
3. **Actionable Next Step**: Which specific C-Level skill (`/prioritize-roadmap`, `/pricing-model`, etc.) or D-Level skill to trigger next.
