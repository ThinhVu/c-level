# /growth-vector

> **CMO Principle**: Great products don't sell themselves; they are engineered with distribution built into their core loop.  
> *"If distribution is an afterthought, failure is the default."*

## Purpose & Objective
`/growth-vector` crafts an actionable customer acquisition strategy specifically for the current codebase. Rather than suggesting generic advice like "post on social media," it identifies the unique distribution hooks embedded within the product:
- **In-App Viral / Referral Loops**: Public URLs, "Powered by [Product]" watermarks, collaborative workspaces.
- **Programmatic SEO (pSEO)**: Leveraging database models to generate thousands of indexed, long-tail problem-solving landing pages.
- **Ecosystem & Integration Infiltration**: Listing in plugin marketplaces (Shopify App Store, Raycast, Zapier, Chrome Web Store).
- **Targeted Community GTM**: Show HN, Reddit subreddits, Discord communities where your ICP actively seeks tools.

## When to Reach for It
- Product has functional utility but zero organic traffic.
- Preparing for a major v1.0 or v2.0 feature release.
- Redesigning the public landing page to increase visitor-to-signup conversion.
- Triggered by typing `/growth-vector`.

---

## Output Deliverable
Generated at `c-suite/growth-playbook.md` using `templates/growth-playbook.md.template`:
- Unfair distribution channel analysis based on product features.
- Step-by-step engineering specs for in-app PLG loops.
- Programmatic SEO keyword templates and URL structures.
- 30-Day Growth Experiment Calendar with measurable targets.
